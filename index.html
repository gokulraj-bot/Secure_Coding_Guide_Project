Safe AI workflow

1. Ask

Request an explanation and defensive fix. Ask for assumptions and official sources.

2. Verify

Check OWASP, NIST, MDN and official language/framework docs. Confirm versions.

3. Test

Compile, test normal and invalid inputs, and review authorization and error paths.

4. Document

Record prompts, sources, corrections, limitations and the date checked.

Ethics

Use only your own local application or an authorized training lab. Do not test real websites without permission. Never share secrets or personal data with public AI tools.
